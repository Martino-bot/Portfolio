About me
Martijn Coenen · Cinematographer & Filmmaker

I'm Martijn Coenen, a cinematographer and filmmaker in training based in Belgium. I'm doing a Master in Film & TV at LUCA School of Arts in Genk. My work sits between intimate non-fiction, narrative shorts and automotive/commercial pieces. I like staying close to people and situations – my grandmother in her living room, someone having a rough day, a soldier alone in a field in Red Tape. I'm more interested in images that feel honest than images that just look perfect.

Sometimes I feel that on set we're always chasing "perfect light", but for me overexposure, low dynamic range or noise aren't automatically wrong. I'd rather build a simple setup that gives the right feeling than force a textbook three-point scheme on every frame. Alongside school I co-founded ChargeLink, a practical on-set system that tracks camera batteries in real time so crews don't lose takes to dead packs, and I run Coenenvideo, a long-running stop-motion and automotive playground where I learned a lot by just making things.

On set, if I'm in charge, I try to keep things calm and straightforward. I want people to feel genuinely respected, not talked over or forced into a fake "good vibe". Outside of film my life is not some perfect feed either: I waste time on YouTube, stress about deadlines, like cars, long drives, messing with gear and spending time with my grandmother. I'm mainly interested in growing as a director of photography and working with people who care more about what's in front of the camera than about the "perfect" setup.

I make films, keep set tech behaving, and look for shots that actually mean something.
