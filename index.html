How it works

10–20 batteries on a modern set and no simple way to watch them — one forgotten swap can stop a take. ChargeLink clips a small wireless node onto every battery and streams each voltage back to one central hub, so the whole set's power is visible at a glance.

  • CommsLoRa · 868 MHz (sub-GHz)
  • RangeUp to 400 m
  • MountsV-Mount · Gold-Mount · B-Mount
  • HubTouchscreen · status LEDs
  • NodesWireless · clip-on · brand-agnostic

About me
Martijn Coenen · Junior DOP / Creative Technologist

I'm Martijn Coenen, a junior DOP and creative technologist based between Belgium and France. I'm doing a Master in Film & Television at LUCA School of Arts in Genk, but a lot of my real schooling happens on set and at my workbench. I bridge two worlds that usually stay apart — cinematic storytelling and on-set innovation: one day operating a camera, the next building hardware for the camera department or wiring up an AI workflow to take the boring parts out of production.

I co-founded ChargeLink, a radio-frequency battery monitoring system for camera assistants — I handle the system architecture, the software and the UI, test it with real ACs and pitch it to industry partners, and we're part of the VRT Seeds & Growth programme and KBC Start it. Before that I grew Coenenvideo from a quiet school period into an automotive video account with millions of views and freelance work for brands like Audi and Aston Martin. Alongside that I assist camera on live broadcasts with NEP and DB Video, and this summer I shadowed the Engineer in Charge at NEP Switzerland during the UEFA Women's Euro.

I'm a continuous, self-directed learner — I'd rather master a new system than wait for someone to hand me a workflow. I'm not chasing "perfect light" either: overexposure, low dynamic range or noise aren't automatically wrong to me; I'd rather build a simple setup that gives the right feeling. And my life isn't a perfect feed — I waste time on YouTube, stress about deadlines, love cars and long drives, mess with gear, and spend time with my grandmother. Mostly I want to keep growing as a DOP while working with people who care more about what's in front of the camera than about the "perfect" setup.

Junior DOP and creative technologist — I shoot films, build the gear and AI tools that support them, and look for shots that actually mean something.
